Print these and put them on the wall

Four A4 sheets — two posters, two sheets of hand-out cards. Everything is sized for a real printer — print at 100%, do not tick “fit to page”, or the QR codes shrink and stop scanning reliably.

  1. Sheet 1 — the donor poster (Become a blood donor). Nothing to cut. Goes on the blood-bank window, every registration desk and the OPD corridor. One code, 13cm across, in the middle of the sheet — wide enough to scan from several metres, so it still works above a counter or behind window glass.
  2. Sheet 2 — the camp poster (Host a camp). Same size code, for the notice board, college and office reception, gram panchayat and club room.
  3. Sheet 3 — eight donor cards. Cut into eight. Hand one to anyone who says “I’ll do it later”, or keep a stack at the reception counter and in the OPD.
  4. Sheet 4 — eight camp cards. Same, for principals, HR managers, sarpanches and club secretaries. One card in the hand beats a phone number on a slip.

Both codes point at raktify.choudhari.ngo — your own domain, no third-party shortener, so nothing between the scanner and the page can log the scan, change the destination, or expire. They keep working as long as the site is up.

Printed in black at the highest available error-correction level: a code can lose up to 30% of its surface — a tear, a tape mark, a sun-faded corner, a trim a millimetre too deep — and still scan.

Type it once and it appears at the top of both posters and both card sheets, above the Raktify mark — the sheet is theirs, the service is ours. Use the name they use on their own board, in Marathi or English. The saved PDF is named after them too, so one folder can hold a sheet per institution. Leave it blank and nothing at all is added, exactly as before. Nothing is uploaded anywhere; the name stays in this browser.
