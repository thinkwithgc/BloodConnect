Print these and put them on the wall

Three A4 sheets. Everything is sized for a real printer — print at 100%, do not tick “fit to page”, or the QR codes shrink and stop scanning reliably.

  1. Sheet 1 — two window posters. Print, then cut along the dashed line in the middle. The top half (Become a blood donor) goes on the blood-bank window and every registration desk. The bottom half (Host a camp) goes on the notice board, OPD corridor, college and office reception.
  2. Sheet 2 — eight donor cards. Cut into eight. Hand one to anyone who says “I’ll do it later”, or keep a stack at the reception counter and in the OPD.
  3. Sheet 3 — eight camp cards. Same, for principals, HR managers, sarpanches and club secretaries. One card in the hand beats a phone number on a slip.

Both codes point at raktify.choudhari.ngo — your own domain, no third-party shortener, so nothing between the scanner and the page can log the scan, change the destination, or expire. They keep working as long as the site is up.

Printed in black at the highest available error-correction level: a code can lose up to 30% of its surface — a tear, a tape mark, a sun-faded corner, a trim a millimetre too deep — and still scan.
