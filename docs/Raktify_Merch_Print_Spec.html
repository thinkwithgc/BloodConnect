01

Read this first

Non-negotiable

The identity is locked

Do not redraw, retype, recolour, outline, stretch or add anything to the Raktify wordmark. It is supplied as vector artwork and must be placed exactly as supplied. A version set in a typeface you have on your system is not the wordmark — the letterforms and the gap around the droplet will not match, and the job will be rejected at sample.

The mark carries ™, never ®. The trade mark application is pending, and printing ® before registration is an offence under §107 of the Trade Marks Act. Never enlarge or restyle the ™ glyph — it is already correctly sized and positioned inside the file.

Everything on this sheet protects one idea: the branding is quiet. This is not event swag with a big logo across the chest. It is a garment somebody chooses to wear on an ordinary day — which is the only reason a stranger ever asks what Raktify is. Restraint is the specification, not a cost saving.

  • ✓Artwork is supplied as .svg plus a print-ready .pdf and a 300 dpi .png. Use the vector for every process.
  • ✓Scale the wordmark by height and let the width follow at a ratio of 3.135 : 1. Never scale the two axes independently.
  • ✓Clear space on all four sides is half the wordmark’s height, minimum. Nothing enters it — no seam, pocket, size label or second mark.
  • ×No outlines, keylines, drop shadows, bevels, gradients, glows, metallic foil or puff.
  • ×No slogans, dates, sponsor logos, hashtags, QR codes or camp names anywhere on the garment.
  • ×No medical crosses, syringes, IV bags, ECG lines, hearts, awareness ribbons, or blood rendered as a wet or glossy liquid.
  • ×Never pair the wordmark with the Raktify app-icon square. The wordmark already contains the droplet; the pair duplicates it.
02

Colour

Match to draw-down

Five values, and nothing else. No blues, greens, purples or cool greys appear anywhere on Raktify merchandise — including labels, tags, thread, webbing and packaging.

Cream

#fdf8f4

Primary garment body. Warm off-white — never blue-white or optic white.

Sand

#f5ece4

Secondary garment body. Overshirt and tote alternative.

Warm ink

#1c1917

Dark garment option, and the ify half of the wordmark. Near-black with a warm cast, not pure black.

Brand red

#b8231a

The Rakt half of the wordmark, and the only accent. Deep, warm, slightly earthen.

Droplet red

#dc2f1d

The droplet only — the dot of the i. One step brighter than brand red, by design.

Screen red always prints hotter than it looks

Treat the hex values as the reference, not the target. Produce a physical draw-down or stitch-out against the supplied printed swatch and get written approval before running the order. Candidate Pantone Solid Coated references to start from are 7621 C and 1805 C — both need judging on the actual substrate, because neither is an exact match.

For thread, match to the printed swatch in daylight. Never select a thread number off a screen. Polyester embroidery thread runs noticeably more saturated than the same value in water-based or discharge ink.

05

Process

Two finishes, one look

Embroidery — preferred

Satin stitch, flat. No puff, no 3D foam. Density set for a soft hand — the back of the work should not be a stiff plate. Cutaway backing on knit, tearaway on canvas, trimmed close.

Tonal option: thread the same colour as the fabric, so the mark reads only as raised texture. This is the finish the whole design rests on, and it needs a stitch-out before approval — tonal work either looks expensive or looks like a mistake, with very little in between.

Minimum legible droplet height in thread is 8 mm. Do not attempt the wordmark below 40 mm wide in thread — the gap around the droplet in the i closes up. Below that width, use the droplet alone.

Screen print — tote and volume tees

Water-based or discharge ink only. Matte, absorbed into the weave. No plastisol gloss, and no deposit you can feel with a fingertip.

The wordmark is two colours — red #b8231a and warm ink #1c1917 — plus the droplet #dc2f1d. Register them properly, or print the mark in a single colour. Never fake the split with an approximation.

On the undyed canvas, test for ink strike-through and for how far the raw fibre shifts the red. Undyed cotton has a yellow-green cast that pushes brand red toward orange.

Droplet at production sizes

Shown at screen scale for shape reference only. The droplet is a smooth geometric teardrop — rounded base, soft point, no highlight, no outline, no tail.

06

Optional Devanagari treatment

Needs sign-off

रक्त is the word for “blood” — the same syllable already inside the name. Set large, tonal and low-contrast on the upper back, it reads as a watermark a Marathi speaker notices and nobody else does.

रक्त

Typeset from supplied vector only

Set in Noto Sans Devanagari, Medium and supplied to you as outlined vector artwork. Do not retype it, do not substitute a font, and do not let design software re-shape the conjuncts. Devanagari breaks in ways a non-reader cannot see — a mis-set character here is not a typo, it is a garment nobody will wear. Every proof must be read by a Marathi speaker before approval.
